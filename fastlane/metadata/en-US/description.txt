Build your dream garden with Garden Merge! Plant seeds, merge items, and grow olive trees, tomato farms, and elegant fountains.

FEATURES:
• 3 unique merge chains: Olive, Tomato, and Fountain
• Drag-and-drop mechanics on a 7x9 board
• Complete orders to earn gold
• Strategic gameplay with energy management
• Game Center leaderboards and achievements
• Auto-save — pick up where you left off

HOW TO PLAY:
1. Tap the Seed Pouch to create new seeds
2. Drag items of the same level to merge them
3. Produce bigger and more valuable items
4. Complete orders to earn gold
